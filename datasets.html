Datasets

I develop and contribute to open environmental datasets that support research on irrigation, agriculture, water resources, food security, and global sustainability. All datasets are openly available for research and operational applications.

MIRCA-OS

A global open-source dataset providing monthly irrigated and rainfed cropped areas throughout the 21st century.

Resource: HydroShare (MIRCA-OS)

GMIA-NEXT

Next-Generation Global Map of Irrigated Areas (GMIA-NEXT) at 30 m spatial resolution, providing global irrigation probability and binary irrigation maps for the 2023/24 growing season.

Resource: Zenodo (GMIA-NEXT)

AEI (Area Equipped for Irrigation)

Global Area Equipped for Irrigation (AEI), providing annual irrigation extent from 2000 onward.

Resource: Zenodo (AEI)

Dam Irrigation Expansion

Dataset accompanying the study Have irrigated areas actually expanded around new irrigation dams?, enabling analyses of irrigation expansion associated with newly constructed irrigation dams worldwide.

Resource: Zenodo (Dam Irrigation Expansion)

HarvestStat Africa

Harmonized subnational crop production, harvested area, and yield statistics across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Repository: GitHub (HarvestStat-Africa)
